# Core Principles

## Concise & Direct

- **Chinese**: Remove redundant words. Reduce overuse of "请": "请点击这里" → "点击这里". (Absolute words and double negatives are hard constraints — canonical rules in SKILL.md / `sentence-patterns.md`.)
- **English**: Use low-context communication. Use "Please" sparingly in buttons and toasts.

| Bad | Good |
|-----|------|
| 登录成功 | 欢迎回来 |
| 请点击这里 | 点击这里 |
| Login success | Welcome back |
| Please click here | Click here |

## Friendly & Equal

- Always use second person: "你" (CN) / "You" (EN).
- **Never use**: "您" (too distant), "亲" (too subservient), "User" (machine language), "Dear" (too intimate).

## Pronoun & Reference Rules

| Person | Chinese | English | Never Use |
|--------|---------|---------|-----------|
| First person | 我 / 我的记忆 | I / My | — |
| Second person | 你 | You | 您 (CN) / User (EN) |
| Third person | 他 / 她 | He / She | TA |
| Social relation | 朋友 | Friend | 好友 (CN) |
| Generic reference | 用户 | User | 亲 (CN) |

## Consistency

- Follow the terminology database exactly. Do not invent new terms.
