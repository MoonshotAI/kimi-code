### Features

- Running Bash commands and subagents can now be sent to the background from their cards.
- Drag images and videos into the composer as attachments, with preview and upload progress.
- Added message auto-fold and tool-call summary switches under Settings → Advanced.

### Polish

- The right sidebar is now tabbed; terminal and panel toggles stay pinned right.
- Default thinking is now a level picker, and new sessions follow it.

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed memory usage growing after repeatedly switching sessions or toggling the right panel.
- Fixed duplicate, lost, or stale messages after switching sessions or reconnecting.
