Add a `follow_up_behavior` setting (`"queue"` or `"steer"`) and per-message shortcuts (`Ctrl-Enter`, `Cmd-Enter`, and `Ctrl-Q`) to choose whether messages sent during a turn wait in the queue or steer the running turn. Configure the default in `/settings` or `tui.toml`.
